# Red Head Makes

## Style decisions:
-Colour Palette:
iridescent pastel tones (soft pinks, blues, yellows greens) and black text. This will match with my business card design and packaging for products to continue my cohesive feel.
-Hex Codes:
Lilac - #D9CFFB
Pink - #FADADD
Blue - #CFE8F3
Pearl White - #F8F7F4

## Typography:
Script for headings, Sans-serif for body and refined serif for subheadings.
Google Fonts: Great Vibes and Playfair Display

How will the page feel?
Handmade but polished, using the pastel tones and harsher blacks for text will create a soft and warm feel in the background with a defined line in text. All the writing in my website should feel like a conversation rather than a list of facts. On the flip side of that I want simplicity not clutter so short and sweet text. The language should evoke creativity using descriptive words but not too heavy. When talking about customer interactions or finances reliability is key. 

Red Head Makes speaks with warmth, clarity, and creativity.
The tone is modern and minimal, with a focus on craftsmanship and calm confidence.
Every message feels personal, thoughtful, and visually inspired.
